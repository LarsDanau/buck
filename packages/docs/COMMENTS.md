# Buck shared - Comments guidelines

Always document functions, hooks & components with JSDoc comments.
Use regular comments for inline comments.

## Core principles

- All comments should be low level, short and easy to understand
- Never use em dashes or other special characters that a human wont use
- Keep the comments short, 1-2 lines ideally

## JSDoc documentation

- Detailed JSDoc comments explaining the function, hook, component, ...
- Use special JSDoc features such as @params (with explanation), @returns, etc

## Regular comments

- Simple 1-2 lines of comments ideally.
